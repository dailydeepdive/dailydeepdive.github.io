# About Daily Deep Dive

Daily Deep Dive (3D) is a 15-20 minute read published each morning. It's how I build knowledge outside finance and learn to spot the few factors that matter most.

## What's in each briefing

1. **Markets.** The previous day's closes and the main reasons given for the moves, with more than one view where sources disagree.
2. **Politics and world.** Facts only: who said what, with no side taken.
3. **Quick hits.** Short items from science, space and technology, each with a "why it matters" line and a caveat.
4. **Spaced repetition.** A short refresher on two earlier deep dives, so the ideas stick.
5. **The deep dive.** One new topic each day: how it works in plain language, where informed opinion stands, and what it means, good and bad.
6. **Your Call.** What most people expect, the one factor that would change the outcome, and a dated yes/no question that a named public source will settle.

## How it's made

Each briefing is researched and drafted with the help of an AI assistant (Claude) and checked against its sources. Every factual claim carries a numbered citation that links to the source in the References section at the bottom of the post. Figures marked "my own arithmetic" are calculations from the cited numbers, not figures taken from a source.

## The fine print

This is personal research and a learning project. It is not investment, tax or legal advice, and nothing here is a recommendation to buy or sell anything. Views are my own and not those of any employer.
